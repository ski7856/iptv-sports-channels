When people search for "IPTV UK sports channels," they're usually after one very specific thing: a reliable way to watch the UK's main sports broadcasters (Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Eurosport, Premier Sports, and the growing list of streaming-only rights holders) without stitching together four separate subscriptions.
